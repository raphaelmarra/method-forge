# Code of Conduct

Method Forge is built through rigorous disagreement conducted with respect.

Participants must:

- critique claims, methods, evidence, and interfaces rather than people;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest;
- distinguish uncertainty from incompetence or bad faith;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sexualized conduct;
- respect privacy, attribution, professional boundaries, and applicable law;
- help make discussions accessible to contributors with different backgrounds and levels of expertise.

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and participation that violate these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible, with attention to the safety of affected participants.
