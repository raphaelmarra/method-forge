# Research, evidence synthesis, investigation, and claim assurance

Use this catalog when the core problem is framing a research question, collecting primary or public evidence, reviewing literature, appraising and synthesizing evidence, investigating open sources, or managing claim uncertainty. Use `07-data-information-knowledge.md` for data governance, semantics, knowledge management, quality, lineage, and provenance infrastructure.

## Research question and review design

| Candidate | Type and output | Use when | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PICO / PICOC | question-framing frameworks | intervention/effect questions need population, intervention, comparator, outcome, and context | force exploratory, legal, technical, or qualitative questions into a clinical mold |
| PCC | scoping-review question frame | population, concept, and context suit broad evidence mapping | narrow causal effect synthesis |
| SPIDER / SPICE | qualitative/mixed question frames | sample/phenomenon/design/evaluation/research type or setting/perspective/intervention/comparison/evaluation fit | use acronyms without changing search concepts |
| Preregistration / Review Protocol | research governance artifact | criteria and analysis choices should be fixed before outcome knowledge | early exploration when the question is not mature; label deviations rather than hide them |
| Scoping Review | evidence-synthesis method | map concepts, evidence types, volume, and gaps | conclude effectiveness or precise effect size |
| Systematic Review | evidence-synthesis method | a focused high-impact question justifies exhaustive protocolized retrieval/appraisal/synthesis | broad ambiguous question, insufficient resources, or rapidly obsolete evidence without update plan |
| Rapid Review | accelerated synthesis method | decision deadline requires transparent shortcuts from a systematic approach | call it equivalent to a full review or conceal omitted sources/screeners |
| Systematic Map / Evidence Gap Map | evidence landscape method | locate concentrations and gaps across interventions/outcomes | infer effects without synthesis/appraisal |
| Living Review | continuously updated review model | evidence changes rapidly and recurring decisions justify maintenance | stable topic or no ownership/update triggers |
| Realist Review / Synthesis | theory-driven synthesis | understand what works, for whom, in what contexts, through which mechanisms | simple homogeneous effect question |

## Primary, qualitative, and participatory research

| Candidate | Type and output | Use when | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Survey Research | quantitative/qualitative primary method | standardized self-reports across a defined population can answer the question | causal claims from convenience samples, leading questions, or severe nonresponse |
| Semi-Structured Interviews | qualitative elicitation method | experiences, meanings, decisions, and mechanisms require depth with comparability | estimate prevalence or treat recollection as direct observation |
| Focus Groups | group qualitative method | interaction among participants can surface norms, language, agreement, and dissent | sensitive power dynamics, confidentiality, or individual prevalence estimates |
| Ethnography / Contextual Inquiry | observational qualitative family | real work, culture, context, and tacit practices differ from stated procedures | short visits are labeled ethnography or observation occurs without consent/reflexivity |
| Case Study Research | bounded empirical research strategy | understand a contemporary phenomenon deeply in context using multiple evidence sources | generalize statistically from one convenient anecdote |
| Grounded Theory | qualitative methodology | build theory iteratively from data through coding, comparison, sampling, and saturation | use “grounded” to mean no prior thinking or mix isolated coding techniques incoherently |
| Thematic Analysis | qualitative analysis method family | identify and interpret patterns across textual/qualitative data | report topic counts without transparent coding/reflexivity or claim one canonical variant |
| Qualitative Content Analysis | systematic analysis method | categorize manifest/latent content with a defined coding frame and context | strip meaning from excerpts or confuse frequency with importance |
| Delphi Method | structured expert elicitation method | uncertain questions benefit from anonymous iterative judgments and controlled feedback | expert consensus is treated as factual truth or panel selection is opaque |
| Nominal Group Technique | structured group-prioritization method | participants need balanced idea generation, clarification, and ranking in one session | anonymity across rounds or complex evidence synthesis is needed |
| Action Research | participatory inquiry/change methodology | practitioners and researchers iteratively diagnose, act, evaluate, and learn | evaluator independence or stable intervention is required and dual roles create unmanaged conflicts |
| Design Science Research | artifact-centered research methodology | build and rigorously evaluate an artifact intended to solve a relevant problem | product development alone without research contribution/evaluation |
| Citizen Science / Participatory Research | participation model family | affected communities should shape questions, data, interpretation, and action | extract unpaid labor, tokenize participation, or omit data/benefit governance |
| Mixed Methods | research design family | integrated quantitative and qualitative evidence answers complementary parts of one question | run two disconnected studies and label them mixed methods |

For qualitative work, declare sampling logic, reflexivity, coding approach, negative cases, audit trail, and the meaning of “saturation.” Do not use statistical representativeness language unless the design supports it.

## Search, selection, appraisal, and synthesis

| Candidate | Type and output | Use when | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PRISMA | reporting guideline | transparently report why/how a systematic review was conducted and what was found | use as the review method, search strategy, or quality appraisal tool |
| PRISMA-ScR | scoping-review reporting extension | transparently report scoping reviews | claim methodological rigor from checklist completion alone |
| PRESS | peer review of search strategies | high-stakes database queries need independent structured review | a replacement for running/piloting searches across sources |
| Citation Chaining | search technique | backward/forward relationships can find terminology and missed evidence | only follow popular citation clusters or ignore retractions/versioning |
| Grey Literature Search | search strategy | publication/availability bias and institutional evidence matter | treat unreviewed material as equal-quality without appraisal |
| Dual Screening / Extraction | error-control method | selection/extraction errors materially affect high-stakes conclusions | low-risk scan where the cost is disproportionate; disclose single-reviewer limitations |
| Risk-of-Bias Tools | design-specific appraisal methods | internal validity must be assessed using a tool matched to study design | one generic checklist for all evidence or total score that hides critical bias |
| Narrative Synthesis | synthesis method | evidence is heterogeneous but patterns, mechanisms, and confidence can be structured | unstructured source-by-source summary without comparative framework |
| Meta-Analysis | statistical synthesis method | sufficiently comparable effect estimates and models justify pooling | heterogeneity, bias, dependence, or incompatible estimands make a pooled number misleading |
| Qualitative Evidence Synthesis | synthesis family | themes, experiences, mechanisms, and contextual findings need systematic integration | convert qualitative frequency into effect size |
| Triangulation | evidence strategy | independent methods/sources/perspectives can test convergence and contradiction | count sources derived from the same origin as independent corroboration |
| GRADE | certainty assessment framework | rate a body of evidence for effect estimates/outcomes | generic credibility score for arbitrary web pages |
| GRADE-CERQual | confidence framework for qualitative findings | assess methodological limitations, coherence, adequacy, and relevance of qualitative syntheses | quantitative effect evidence or unsynthesized anecdotes |

## Open-source and digital investigations

| Candidate | Type and output | Use when | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Berkeley Protocol on Digital Open Source Investigations | professional investigation protocol | public digital material must be identified, collected, preserved, verified, analyzed, and reported with professional safeguards | claim full protocol compliance outside its human-rights/legal context when only selected technical fragments are used |
| OSINT Collection Plan | investigation artifact | questions, indicators, source channels, queries, timing, authorities, and stopping rules need control | indiscriminate browsing or collection beyond lawful/ethical scope |
| Digital Source Verification | verification technique family | authenticity, source, location, time, integrity, and content require separate tests | one metadata field, reverse-image match, or visual plausibility is treated as proof |
| Geolocation / Chronolocation | verification techniques | a digital item must be tied to place and time through independent landmarks, shadows, weather, metadata, archives, or corroboration | accuracy exceeds available resolution or present-day imagery is projected backward |
| Web Archiving / Preservation | evidence-preservation practice | volatile pages and files need capture date, version, content, headers/context, and integrity records | archive availability is confused with authenticity or lawful admissibility |
| Source Lineage / Independence Analysis | evidence technique | many apparent sources may derive from one release, witness, dataset, or image | count repetition as independent corroboration |
| Temporal–Spatial Claim Matrix | local investigation artifact | historical facts require explicit location precision, event period, publication date, and capture date | current existence is inferred to a historical date without continuity evidence |

For historical investigations, split existence, installation, operation, coverage, and quality into separate claims. Absence of search results refutes a claim only when the searched register is demonstrably complete for that exact attribute, place, and period.

## Claims, evidence, and uncertainty

| Candidate | Type and output | Use when | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Claim–Evidence Matrix | evidence artifact | material claims need sources, inference, status, conflicts, and gaps | source count is treated as strength without relevance/quality/independence |
| Evidence Hierarchy | contextual appraisal heuristic | study designs differ in protection from particular biases | universal pyramid that ignores question, execution, directness, and synthesis |
| Source Criticism / CRAAP-like checks | source-evaluation techniques | authorship, currency, purpose, corroboration, and primary status need screening | reduce credibility to a simplistic score or ignore claim-specific evidence |
| Analysis of Competing Hypotheses (ACH) | diagnostic reasoning method | multiple explanations must be tested against discriminating evidence | evidence independence/diagnosticity is weak or matrices create false objectivity |
| Bayesian Updating | probabilistic evidence method | priors and likelihoods can be made explicit and updated | hide subjective priors or fabricate numerical likelihoods |
| Epistemic Status / Claim Lifecycle | evidence-governance pattern | distinguish candidate, supported, verified, conflicting, rejected, and stale claims | one undifferentiated “confidence” score |
| Data / Evidence Saturation Rule | stopping protocol | open-ended research needs explicit coverage and marginal-gain criteria | claim universal web exhaustiveness |

## Research anchors

- Cochrane and JBI manuals; PRISMA family; PRESS; GRADE and CERQual.
- UN OHCHR/Berkeley Protocol and Berkeley Human Rights Center guidance for digital open-source preservation and verification; tailor explicitly outside the original legal/human-rights scope.
- Match appraisal and synthesis methods to the question and evidence design. Record protocol deviations and negative searches.
- Use `07-data-information-knowledge.md` for provenance models, integrity mechanisms, data quality, semantic organization, and knowledge-management systems that support this work.
