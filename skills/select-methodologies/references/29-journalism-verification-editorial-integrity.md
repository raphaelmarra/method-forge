# Journalism, verification, fact-checking, and editorial integrity

Use this catalog when producing, verifying, editing, correcting, or publishing factual public-interest material; when investigating claims and actors; or when a newsroom/editorial process must protect independence, sources, accuracy, fairness, safety, and accountability. Journalism methods organize evidence and publication decisions; they do not grant investigative authority or override law.

## Assignment and reporting design

| Candidate | Type and output | Use when | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Editorial assignment brief | governance artifact; question, public interest, scope, audience, deliverable, deadline, risks, standards | reporter/editor need shared expectations and boundaries | desired narrative/conclusion is fixed before reporting |
| Reporting hypothesis | investigative planning artifact | a testable provisional explanation can guide evidence collection | hypothesis becomes accusation, confirmation target, or published fact |
| Story map / reporting plan | planning artifact; claims, sources, documents, scenes, data, gaps, deadlines | complex reporting needs coordinated coverage and dependencies | plan freezes emerging evidence or activity count replaces decisive proof |
| Public-interest test | editorial decision framework | privacy, intrusion, covert methods, naming, and publication need justification | audience curiosity or clicks are called public interest |
| Stakeholder / source map | investigation representation | actors, roles, interests, knowledge, documents, relationships, and missing voices matter | association is treated as culpability or sensitive map is insecure |
| Source matrix | evidence artifact; claim, source type, access, independence, reliability, corroboration, risk | material claims depend on multiple sources and provenance | source count replaces independence, directness, and quality |
| Timeline / chronology | temporal evidence artifact | sequence, opportunity, contradictions, and causal claims depend on dates | proximity implies causation or timezone/version uncertainty is hidden |
| Questions-to-prove / reporting checklist | gap-control artifact | allegations or explanatory claims need explicit evidentiary elements | checklist encourages tunnel vision and excludes disconfirming evidence |
| Right-of-reply protocol | fairness/accountability process | criticized parties need specific claims, evidence basis, time, and meaningful response opportunity | vague last-minute request performs fairness without usable time/context |
| Trauma-informed reporting plan | harm-aware method | victims/survivors and vulnerable people may face retraumatization, stigma, or danger | care becomes paternalism or factual scrutiny is abandoned |

## Source, document, data, and digital verification

| Candidate | Type and output | Use when | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SIFT | rapid source-checking heuristic; stop, investigate source, find better coverage, trace context | fast online claims need lateral reading and provenance | high-stakes investigation stops at quick checks |
| Lateral reading | web-verification method | evaluate source reputation, ownership, expertise, and external coverage | search-engine ranking/consensus is treated as truth |
| Primary-source tracing | provenance method | locate original document, data, statement, image, or study behind a claim | “primary” source is assumed truthful, complete, or independent |
| Triangulation | evidence method | independent source types can test the same material claim | correlated repetition or common upstream source is counted as confirmation |
| Source reliability / information credibility separation | analytic discipline | distinguish who supplied information from whether this claim is corroborated | one global source label determines every statement |
| Document authentication | forensic verification workflow | origin, integrity, date, author, custody, metadata, signatures, and content consistency matter | appearance or metadata alone proves authenticity |
| Chain of custody / evidence log | provenance artifact | sensitive leaked, physical, or digital material must remain traceable and unchanged | journalistic log is represented as legal forensic custody without required controls |
| Version comparison / document diff | change-analysis method | edits, omissions, revisions, and timing may be material | formatting changes are overinterpreted or versions are incomplete |
| Data provenance audit | data-journalism method | source, collection, definitions, transformations, missingness, revisions, and access shape claims | official data is assumed neutral/complete or dashboard export lacks definitions |
| Reproducible data analysis | computational reporting practice | transformations, calculations, charts, and results should be reviewable and rerunnable | code reproducibility proves data validity or protects sensitive data automatically |
| Image reverse search | visual-verification technique | prior appearances and original context may identify reuse/misattribution | no match means authentic or earliest indexed copy is original |
| Video keyframe / sequence verification | visual method | isolate scenes for provenance, chronology, edits, and location checks | compression/artifacts are treated as definitive manipulation evidence |
| Geolocation | OSINT verification method | landmarks, terrain, signs, shadows, maps, imagery, and local sources can locate media | uncertain clues expose vulnerable location or approximate match is called exact |
| Chronolocation | temporal verification method | weather, shadows, events, celestial/traffic/context clues can bound capture time | post time equals capture time or one ambiguous clue decides |
| Metadata examination | technical verification technique | EXIF/file/container/header data may support provenance and editing history | metadata is easily stripped/altered and never conclusive alone |
| Deepfake / synthetic-media assessment | multimodal verification workflow | content origin/manipulation is material and requires tools plus contextual corroboration | detector score alone proves authenticity or falsity |
| Archive / cache verification | web-history method | deleted/changed pages and publication timing matter | archive absence proves page never existed or snapshot is complete |

## Investigation and fact-checking

| Candidate | Type and output | Use when | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Investigative reporting cycle | backbone; tip, hypothesis, evidence, verification, response, review, publication, follow-up | concealed systemic conduct or accountability issue requires sustained reporting | suspicion is published before corroboration, legality, safety, and fairness review |
| Open-source investigation — OSINT | source/method family | legally accessible public information can reconstruct actors, events, assets, networks, and claims | access authorization, privacy, doxxing, security, or overclaiming is unmanaged |
| Records-request strategy | document acquisition method | public records laws/processes can obtain official evidence | request substitutes for wider sourcing or jurisdiction/exemptions/deadlines are ignored |
| Follow-the-money | investigative method | flows, entities, ownership, contracts, payments, and benefits may reveal relationships | transaction/association implies illegality without purpose, authority, and context |
| Corporate/entity network analysis | investigative representation | ownership, directors, affiliates, addresses, filings, and transactions need connection | name/address matches are treated as identity without resolution |
| Social-network analysis | structural method | patterns of coordination, amplification, or relationships need systematic analysis | follows/centrality imply intent or culpability and missing data is ignored |
| Event reconstruction | multi-source chronology method | fragmented accounts/media/documents can reconstruct what happened | synchronized timeline hides uncertainty and conflicting clocks |
| Fact-checking workflow | claim-verification backbone; claim, source, evidence, context, verdict/explanation | a specific checkable public claim materially affects understanding | opinion/value/prediction is forced into binary truth rating |
| Claim decomposition | analytic technique | compound rhetoric contains several factual, causal, comparative, and normative parts | fragmented checking misses overall misleading implication |
| Evidence hierarchy for fact-checking | selection heuristic | prefer direct, authoritative, current, independent, methodologically fit evidence | official source is automatically correct or hierarchy overrides context |
| Rating scale / verdict taxonomy | communication artifact | newsroom needs consistent labels for supported, false, misleading, unproven, outdated, etc. | label oversimplifies uncertainty or readers see verdict without evidence |
| Context verification | interpretive method | technically true fragment may mislead through denominator, timeframe, quote, image, or omission | “context” becomes subjective excuse without evidence |
| Causal-claim check | evidence method | claims that X caused Y need design, mechanism, alternatives, timing, and uncertainty | correlation, expert quote, or before/after establishes causality; route to `08` |
| Statistical claim check | quantitative verification | denominators, uncertainty, base rates, definitions, comparisons, and revisions matter | recalculation alone validates sampling/model/measurement |
| Adversarial / disconfirming search | bias-control method | actively seek evidence that would falsify or weaken leading account | false balance gives fringe unsupported claims equal weight |

## Writing, editing, and publication

| Candidate | Type and output | Use when | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Inverted pyramid | news-writing structure | essential verified facts should lead under time/space constraints | investigation/explainer needs chronology, narrative, or cumulative evidence |
| Nut graf | explanatory writing device | readers need why the story matters, scope, and central question early | paragraph asserts significance beyond evidence |
| Scene–evidence–context structure | narrative reporting pattern | human experience and documented analysis must alternate clearly | vivid anecdote is generalized or dramatization blurs fact |
| Explanatory journalism model | synthesis approach | complex system, policy, science, or process needs mechanisms, context, and implications | explainer launders contested assumptions as settled fact |
| Data-story workflow | analytical communication method | data supports a public-interest question through analysis, visualization, and narrative | available dataset drives story or chart implies unsupported causality |
| Attribution protocol | editorial standard | readers must know source, status, access, and uncertainty of material claims | vague “experts say” or source laundering hides origin/conflicts |
| Anonymous-source protocol | high-risk sourcing process | material information cannot be obtained on record and source identity/risk can be editor-verified | anonymity is convenience, unsupported attack, or reader cannot assess motivation |
| Quote verification | accuracy/fairness method | wording, context, translation, transcription, and edits may materially change meaning | source approval becomes editorial veto or polished quote is presented verbatim |
| Headline/deck integrity check | publication assurance | compression must preserve evidence, uncertainty, subject, scale, and causal status | click optimization overstates article or headline is reviewed separately from evidence |
| Visual evidence captioning | context/attribution method | images/video/graphics need who/what/when/where/source/edit status | stock/illustrative media appears evidentiary or crop removes material context |
| Prepublication fact check | independent verification process | consequential long-form/investigative work needs claim-by-claim evidence review | fact checker lacks source access/time/independence or edit changes bypass recheck |
| Legal review | specialist assurance gate | defamation, privacy, contempt, copyright, source protection, court orders, and risk are material | lawyer becomes editor/truth arbiter or review is sought after irreversible publication |
| Standards editor / editorial review | governance role/process | high-risk ethics, fairness, methods, conflicts, and precedent need independent judgment | standards review is nominal, late, or subordinate to commercial pressure |

## Ethics, safety, corrections, and assurance

| Candidate | Type and output | Use when | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Harm-minimization assessment | ethics gate | naming, imagery, detail, location, methods, and timing may expose people to harm | discomfort to powerful actors is equated with harm or public interest disappears |
| Source-risk assessment | safety artifact | retaliation, legal, digital, physical, employment, immigration, and trauma risks need mitigation | promises of absolute anonymity/security exceed capability |
| Secure source communication | operational security method | sensitive sources/material need threat-modeled channels, access, retention, and contingency | a fashionable encrypted app is assumed sufficient |
| Conflict-of-interest disclosure/recusal | governance control | financial, personal, political, source, sponsor, or prior-role interests may affect work | disclosure is treated as cure where recusal/independent review is required |
| Editorial independence firewall | governance model | owners, advertisers, funders, partners, and commercial teams could influence coverage | formal policy lacks enforcement, transparency, and protected escalation |
| Corrections policy and workflow | accountability process | errors need intake, severity triage, transparent correction, propagation, notification, and learning | silent edit erases accountability or every cosmetic edit becomes alarming correction |
| Retraction / withdrawal | exceptional remedy | core story/evidence is irreparably false, unsafe, unlawful, or compromised | removal hides history without notice/archive and leaves syndicated copies uncorrected |
| Living-story update protocol | versioned publishing method | facts evolve and readers need timestamps, changes, current status, and preserved context | updates overwrite original claims and chronology silently |
| Post-publication monitoring | assurance process | new evidence, harm, corrections, legal responses, and impact require follow-up | engagement/reaction is treated as truth or monitoring enables harassment |
| Editorial postmortem | learning method | major error, near miss, investigation, or impact needs causal process review | blame suppresses reporting or confidential source details leak |
| Accuracy/fairness/source-diversity metrics | balanced measurement family | editorial system needs sensors for corrections, claim support, representation, timeliness, and gaps | quotas/low correction count become targets and distort judgment |

## Composition patterns

### Verify a viral claim

`decompose claim → preserve original/provenance → investigate source → trace primary material → lateral/independent corroboration → image/video/time/location checks → context/statistical/causal review → contact claimant/affected parties → evidence-linked explanation → correction monitoring`

### Conduct a public-interest investigation

`assignment/public-interest test → hypothesis + disconfirmers → actor/source/document map → legal/safety/data plan → records/interviews/OSINT/data → provenance + timeline + claim matrix → right of reply → independent fact/legal/standards review → publish with methods/uncertainty → follow-up/corrections`

### Publish data journalism

`public-interest question → source/provenance/definitions → reproducible cleaning/analysis → uncertainty and alternative explanations → representative cases/context → visual integrity/accessibility → independent data/fact check → publish data/method where safe → revision monitoring`

## Research anchors and status

Status checked 2026-08-13. Editorial codes differ by newsroom and jurisdiction; verify controlling law, court restrictions, source protection, privacy, copyright, security, and professional standards.

- [UNESCO Journalism, “Fake News” & Disinformation handbook](https://www.unesco.org/pt/articles/jornalismo-fake-news-desinformacao) covers information disorder, fact-checking, and social-media verification and is available in Portuguese.
- Use primary newsroom/editorial codes and current verification handbooks for attribution, anonymous sources, corrections, visual verification, and prepublication review; distinguish policy from empirical efficacy evidence.
- Route general evidence/provenance/review methods to `07`, causal/statistical analysis to `08`, legal investigations to `13`, communication/content distribution to `27`, and digital security to `06`.
- OSINT means information from legally accessible sources, not permission to intrude, deceive, dox, access systems, or repurpose sensitive data unlawfully.
