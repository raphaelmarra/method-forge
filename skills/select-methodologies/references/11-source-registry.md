## Source hierarchy

For a candidate, search in this order:

1. normative specification or standards-body page;
2. official maintainer/author guide and revision history;
3. seminal peer-reviewed paper or primary handbook;
4. independent empirical evaluation, systematic review, or documented implementation;
5. critical/limitation literature and known failure cases;
6. practitioner summaries only for discovery or examples.

Record `owner`, `canonical URL`, `stable version`, `status`, `publication date`, `checked_at`, and `supersedes/superseded_by`. Never use the search engine's displayed date as the standard's publication date without opening the owner page.
