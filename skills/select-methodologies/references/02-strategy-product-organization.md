# Strategy, product, organization, and change

Use this catalog when the problem is primarily about direction, value, discovery, prioritization, stakeholders, organization design, adoption, or governance. Verify current owner guidance and versions before deep adoption.

## Strategy and outcomes

| Candidate | Type and output | Use when | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Theory of Change / Logic Model | causal program model: inputs → activities → outputs → outcomes → impact | stakeholders need explicit causal assumptions and measurable outcomes | a simple execution plan is already known; do not mistake assumed arrows for causal proof |
| Strategy Map / Balanced Scorecard | strategy-to-objective and measurement framework | translate strategy across financial, customer, process, and capability perspectives | discovery is still too early or measures will become disconnected targets |
| OKRs | goal-setting framework: objectives and measurable key results | teams need aligned outcome direction with periodic learning | business-as-usual task tracking or compensation formula based mechanically on scores |
| Hoshin Kanri | strategy-deployment method | a few breakthrough priorities must align vertically and horizontally with feedback | rapidly shifting exploratory work without stable strategic direction |
| Wardley Mapping | situational strategy-mapping technique | value chain, user need, component evolution, and build/buy/commodity choices matter | quantitative forecasting or a map built without domain evidence |
| Scenario Planning | strategic foresight method | multiple plausible external futures could change today's robust choices | selecting one forecast and calling it a scenario exercise |
| PESTLE | environmental scanning framework | political, economic, social, technological, legal, and environmental forces need coverage | causal, competitive, or operational analysis by itself |
| Porter's Five Forces | industry-structure framework | competitive pressure and profit-pool structure are central | internal execution, non-market ecosystems, or fast-changing complement networks without extensions |
| SWOT / TOWS | synthesis heuristic / strategy-matching matrix | consolidate verified internal and external factors into strategic options | use as the primary research method or fill with opinions |
| Business Model Canvas / Lean Canvas | business-model artifact | expose customer, value, channel, resource, revenue, and cost hypotheses | investment proof without unit economics, market evidence, and risks |

## Product and service discovery

| Candidate | Type and output | Use when | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Double Diamond | discovery/design framework | explore and converge on problem and solution spaces | control or assurance backbone; detailed design-method selection belongs to `20-design-experience-communication.md` |
| Design Thinking | human-centered innovation family | needs, desirability, prototypes, and iterative learning are central | use as generic branding without field research; detailed design-method selection belongs to `20-design-experience-communication.md` |
| Human-Centred Design / ISO 9241-210 | lifecycle principles and activities | interactive systems need explicit user context, evaluation, and iteration | assume user preference overrides safety, feasibility, or accessibility constraints; HCD execution belongs to `20` |
| Service Design / Service Blueprint | cross-channel service method/artifact | frontstage, backstage, people, systems, and failure points must align | a purely internal algorithm with no service journey; service-design execution belongs to `20` |
| Jobs to Be Done | needs/framing lens | understand progress sought and switching context beyond demographics | replace observation or treat one interview quote as a stable “job” |
| Continuous Discovery | product operating practice | a product team can run frequent customer learning tied to decisions | fixed non-negotiable scope with no capacity to act on learning |
| Opportunity Solution Tree | product-discovery artifact | connect an outcome to opportunities, solutions, and experiments | present the tree as evidence rather than a hypothesis map |
| Lean Startup / Build–Measure–Learn | experimentation lifecycle | key value/growth hypotheses can be tested cheaply and reversibly | unsafe, regulated, irreversible “MVPs,” or tests without falsifiable hypotheses |
| Design Sprint | timeboxed discovery/prototype method | a focused problem needs rapid alignment and user feedback | deep domain research or production delivery; tailoring belongs to `20` |
| Dual-Track Agile | product workflow pattern | discovery and delivery must run continuously with synchronized learning | two isolated teams with handoff and no shared outcomes |
| Pretotyping / smoke tests | demand-testing techniques | test whether users will act before building expensive capability | deceptive tests, safety-critical behavior, or when technical feasibility is the main uncertainty |

## Systems thinking and complex problems

| Candidate | Type and output | Use when | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Soft Systems Methodology (SSM) | learning/action methodology | multiple worldviews make the problem itself contested and purposeful activity models can structure learning | seek one objectively optimal technical design or use rich pictures as decorative diagrams |
| CATWOE | SSM analysis mnemonic | clarify customers, actors, transformation, worldview, owners, and environmental constraints of a purposeful system | use as a complete method or assume one worldview is authoritative |
| Rich Pictures | problem-situation representation technique | relationships, conflicts, structures, processes, and concerns need low-formality elicitation | final causal, requirements, or architecture model |
| Critical Systems Heuristics | boundary-critique framework | examine who benefits, controls, supplies expertise, bears harm, and is excluded from system boundaries | a purely technical optimization with agreed legitimate boundaries |
| System Dynamics | feedback modeling/simulation methodology | stocks, flows, delays, nonlinear feedback, and policy resistance shape long-term behavior | an uncalibrated causal-loop diagram is treated as a forecast |
| Causal Loop Diagrams | qualitative systems-modeling technique | feedback hypotheses and reinforcing/balancing loops need elicitation | quantify behavior or prove causal effects without equations/data |
| Viable System Model | organizational cybernetics model | autonomy, coordination, control, intelligence, and policy across recursive organizational levels need diagnosis | simple org-chart redesign or dogmatic mapping of every unit |
| Complex Adaptive Systems lens | theory/lens | emergence, adaptation, local interaction, and path dependence limit command-and-control plans | excuse lack of measurable intervention and learning |
| Cynefin | sense-making framework | response should differ across clear, complicated, complex, chaotic, and confused contexts | label people/projects permanently or select among quantified investments |

## Innovation and solution generation

| Candidate | Type and output | Use when | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TRIZ | systematic inventive problem-solving method family | technical contradictions and recurring invention patterns can generate solution principles | market desirability, organizational adoption, or novices apply jargon mechanically |
| Morphological Analysis | structured option-generation technique | a multidimensional design space needs combinations beyond obvious concepts | dimensions/values are dependent and combination count becomes unmanageable |
| SCAMPER | ideation heuristic | a familiar product/process needs quick prompts to modify, combine, adapt, or rearrange | deep research, validation, or high-novelty technical invention |
| Biomimicry Design Process | innovation method | biological strategies may inspire functional solutions and lifecycle thinking | superficial form imitation or safety claims without engineering validation |
| Design for X (DfX) | design method family | optimize explicitly for manufacturing, assembly, reliability, serviceability, sustainability, cost, or another lifecycle objective | apply one “X” without analyzing trade-offs with other qualities |

## Prioritization and product planning

| Candidate | Type and output | Use when | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RICE | scoring heuristic | comparable initiatives have defensible reach, impact, confidence, and effort | regulatory obligations or invented numbers that create false precision |
| WSJF | economic sequencing heuristic | cost of delay and job size are comparable enough for queue decisions | dependencies and hard constraints dominate or delay estimates are fictional |
| MoSCoW | scope-prioritization heuristic | a fixed timebox needs explicit must/should/could/won't commitments | every stakeholder labels everything “must” or value/risk trade-offs need precision |
| Kano Model | customer-satisfaction classification | distinguish basic, performance, and delight attributes with research | engineering, compliance, or economic prioritization alone |
| Cost of Delay | economic decision concept | timing changes value and sequencing | benefits cannot be time-profiled even approximately |
| Product Roadmapping | communication/planning artifact family | express outcomes, bets, dependencies, and learning horizons | promise feature-date certainty under high uncertainty |

## Organization, governance, and change

| Candidate | Type and output | Use when | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RACI / responsibility assignment | governance artifact | decision and execution roles are genuinely ambiguous | use as a substitute for process redesign or make everyone “consulted” |
| RAPID / DACI | decision-rights frameworks | recurring cross-functional decisions stall over authority | simple local decisions or terminology proliferation |
| Stakeholder Analysis / power–interest mapping | engagement technique | parties have differing influence, impact, knowledge, or resistance | manipulate stakeholders or ignore low-power/high-impact groups |
| ADKAR | individual-change framework | diagnose awareness, desire, knowledge, ability, and reinforcement gaps | organization structure, incentives, or politics are the real cause |
| Kotter 8-Step | organization-change framework | broad transformation needs coalition, vision, mobilization, and anchoring | small local adoption or rigid linear execution |
| Bridges Transition Model | human-transition lens | identity, loss, and psychological transition affect adoption | a substitute for technical enablement or structural change |
| McKinsey 7S | organizational-alignment diagnostic | strategy, structure, systems, skills, staff, style, and shared values may be misaligned | causal proof or detailed implementation method |
| Team Topologies | sociotechnical organization model | cognitive load and team interaction modes shape software flow | non-software organization or copying team types without value-stream analysis |
| Sociotechnical Systems Design | design discipline | joint optimization of people, incentives, process, and technology is necessary | a purely technical component with negligible work-system impact |
| COBIT | enterprise information/technology governance framework | governance objectives, controls, accountability, and assurance must align | a software delivery method or wholesale control adoption without tailoring |
| ISO management systems | governance/management-system family | a domain needs policy, objectives, roles, audits, continual improvement, and possibly certification | a task-level execution recipe; choose the specific standard, not “ISO” generically |

## Research anchors

- Human-centred design: ISO 9241-210.
- Project, programme, and portfolio execution/governance: route to `30-project-programme-portfolio-management.md`.
- Technology governance: ISACA COBIT.
- Product/agile frameworks: canonical guides or original maintainers, not training summaries.
- Change frameworks: original publishers; separately seek independent evidence because commercial prevalence is not efficacy proof.
- Systems methods: original SSM, systems dynamics, critical systems, and organizational cybernetics literature; distinguish qualitative maps from calibrated simulation.
