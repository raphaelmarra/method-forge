# Negotiation, mediation, conflict, and dispute systems

Use this catalog when parties with partly differing interests must reach, implement, repair, or decline an agreement; when a neutral third party may assist; or when recurring disputes need a designed system. Do not use negotiation methods to bypass rights, consent, safety, or controlling law.

## Preparation and diagnosis

| Candidate | Type and output | Use when | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Principled negotiation | negotiation backbone; people/problems, interests, options, criteria | value creation and legitimacy matter beyond positional compromise | abuse, coercion, incapacity, or non-negotiable rights make joint problem solving unsafe |
| BATNA analysis | reservation/alternative method | determine what to do without agreement and improve walk-away strength | alternatives are imaginary, illegal, or used as threats without feasibility |
| WATNA / MLATNA range | risk-scenario variants | consequences of no agreement need downside and likely-case analysis | scenario labels replace probabilities, evidence, and contingency planning |
| Reservation value | decision threshold | compare proposals with a defensible no-deal alternative and costs | one number hides uncertainty, option value, relationships, and implementation risk |
| ZOPA analysis | bargaining-range model | parties' reservation ranges may overlap | reservation points are assumed known or no monetary range captures the issues |
| Interests–positions–needs map | diagnostic artifact | stated demands obscure underlying concerns, constraints, and priorities | psychologizing the other party replaces verification or rights are reframed as preferences |
| Stakeholder / constituency map | representation | negotiators answer to principals, coalitions, veto players, or affected groups | only people at the table are treated as legitimate stakeholders |
| Issue–priority–trade matrix | planning artifact | multiple issues differ in value and tradability across parties | sacred, legal, ethical, or safety constraints are treated as tradable |
| Objective-criteria research | legitimacy/evidence method | standards, precedents, market data, or expert criteria can support fair terms | selectively chosen benchmark launders power or incomparable cases |
| Negotiation mandate | governance artifact; authority, scope, concessions, escalations, approvals | agents negotiate on behalf of organizations or principals | apparent authority exceeds real authority or confidential limits are exposed |
| Conflict-style instruments | self-reflection heuristics | teams need language for competing, accommodating, avoiding, compromising, collaborating | personality labeling predicts behavior or substitutes for context and skill |

## Bargaining and agreement design

| Candidate | Type and output | Use when | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Integrative bargaining | value-creation method | parties value issues differently and can trade across them | fixed single-issue conflict has no compatible interests or information sharing is unsafe |
| Distributive bargaining | value-claiming method | a genuinely fixed divisible issue remains after value creation | adversarial anchoring destroys a relationship or hides expandable issues |
| MESO — Multiple Equivalent Simultaneous Offers | offer-design technique | reveal preferences and create choices without conceding total value | packages are not truly equivalent or complexity is manipulative |
| Logrolling | trade method | issues have different relative priorities | traded terms harm absent stakeholders or create hidden implementation conflicts |
| Contingent agreement | uncertainty-sharing method | parties disagree about future outcomes but can define observable contingencies | measures are gameable, unverifiable, legally problematic, or create perverse incentives |
| Single-text procedure | convergence method | many parties need one evolving draft rather than competing texts | drafter controls substance opaquely or dissent disappears |
| Package bargaining | agreement-structuring method | interdependent terms should be evaluated as a whole | all-or-nothing packaging conceals unacceptable clauses |
| Anchoring with justification | offer tactic | an evidence-based first proposal can frame a legitimate range | extreme unsupported anchors damage trust or exploit cognitive bias |
| Graduated reciprocation in tension reduction — GRIT | de-escalation strategy | repeated conflict may respond to small unilateral verifiable concessions | concession exposes people to harm or opponent exploits without reciprocation safeguards |
| Agreement architecture | implementation artifact; obligations, contingencies, governance, monitoring, remedies, exit | a deal must survive operational reality and change | headline consensus is accepted without authority, feasibility, ambiguity, and enforcement review |
| Post-settlement settlement | improvement technique | after agreement, a protected process may find a Pareto improvement | reopening creates coercion, strategic regret, or undermines finality |

## Mediation and facilitated resolution

| Candidate | Type and output | Use when | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Facilitative mediation | neutral process method | parties can decide outcomes but need structure, communication, and option development | mediator must provide binding decision or safety/power imbalance cannot be managed |
| Evaluative mediation | mediator-evaluation variant | parties explicitly need reality testing informed by domain/legal expertise | mediator opinion compromises neutrality or substitutes for independent advice |
| Transformative mediation | recognition/empowerment approach | relationship interaction and party agency are central | urgent distributive/legal decision needs concrete resolution |
| Narrative mediation | conflict-story reframing approach | dominant narratives lock identities and possible agreements | material power, facts, or rights are reduced to discourse |
| Shuttle diplomacy / caucusing | separate-session technique | direct interaction is blocked, dangerous, or strategically frozen | mediator becomes opaque messenger or manipulates information |
| Joint fact-finding | collaborative evidence method | factual/technical disputes block value or policy negotiation | values are disguised as facts or experts lack independence and agreed questions |
| Reality testing | mediator/facilitator technique | parties need to examine feasibility, alternatives, consequences, and implementation | questioning becomes coercion or unauthorized legal/technical advice |
| Restorative conferencing | harm-repair process | voluntary accountable dialogue can address impact, responsibility, and repair | victim safety, consent, accountability, or qualified facilitation is absent |
| Ombuds process | confidential/impartial organizational mechanism | people need informal navigation, options, trend detection, and escalation routes | ombuds is represented as investigator, advocate, or binding adjudicator beyond mandate |
| Online dispute resolution — ODR | digital process family | high-volume, low-value, geographically dispersed disputes need accessible staged resolution | digital exclusion, identity, confidentiality, complexity, or power asymmetry dominates |

## Conflict analysis and dispute systems

| Candidate | Type and output | Use when | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Conflict tree | causal/problem representation | root causes, core problem, and effects need participatory elicitation | hierarchy oversimplifies feedback, contested causality, or actor incentives |
| Conflict mapping | actor/relationship/issues map | alliances, influence, grievances, resources, and escalation pathways matter | static map is treated as objective and safe to disclose |
| Conflict sensitivity / Do No Harm | intervention-assurance approach | an intervention may interact with dividers, connectors, resources, or legitimacy | slogan replaces contextual analysis and monitoring |
| Conflict assessment framework | structured diagnosis | context, actors, causes, dynamics, and scenarios must inform intervention | external analyst imposes categories without local validation |
| Consensus building | multi-party process | legitimate stakeholders can seek broad agreement on complex shared problems | consensus enables veto abuse, suppresses principled dissent, or authority is unclear |
| Interest-based problem solving | collaborative workplace method | recurring operational conflict can be reframed around shared and differing interests | serious misconduct, rights, or investigation require formal process |
| Dispute System Design — DSD | organizational/system methodology | recurring disputes need prevention, low-cost resolution, escalation, feedback, and redesign | one exceptional dispute or designers ignore power, access, and due process |
| Stepped grievance mechanism | process architecture | complaints need accessible intake, informal/formal routes, appeal, remedy, and learning | internal process blocks regulators/courts, retaliation protection, or urgent safeguarding |
| Early neutral evaluation | dispute triage method | parties benefit from an independent preliminary merits/risk view | opinion entrenches positions, lacks evidence, or is mistaken for judgment |
| Arbitration | private adjudicative process | parties validly choose a binding specialist decision under applicable rules | consent is defective, rights prohibit arbitration, or transparency/public precedent is required |

## Assurance and measurement

| Candidate | Type and output | Use when | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Power and coercion assessment | hard gate | dependency, retaliation, violence, capacity, or information asymmetry may invalidate consent | “neutrality” hides unsafe or involuntary participation |
| Authority and legality check | hard gate | representatives, terms, process, confidentiality, and remedies have legal consequences | negotiator or mediator substitutes for licensed legal advice |
| Agreement quality review | assurance matrix; clarity, feasibility, legitimacy, fairness, durability, adaptability | draft terms need independent challenge before signature | signature itself is treated as success |
| Implementation and compliance review | monitoring process | obligations, milestones, disputes, adaptations, and outcomes continue after agreement | compliance count ignores strategic behavior and underlying purpose |
| Process fairness assessment | evaluation method | voice, neutrality, respect, understanding, and decision quality affect legitimacy | satisfaction alone proves substantive fairness or legality |
| Negotiation learning review | after-action method | teams need to preserve assumptions, moves, outcomes, relationship effects, and lessons | confidential strategy is exposed or outcome bias judges process |
| Dispute-system metrics | measurement family; incidence, access, time, cost, escalation, resolution, recurrence, retaliation, remedy | recurring system performance needs governance | low complaint count is called success despite fear or inaccessible channels |

## Composition patterns

### Prepare and conduct a consequential negotiation

`mandate/authority → stakeholders + interests → BATNA/risk/reservation → issues and objective criteria → package/MESO strategy → process and information rules → bargaining + reality testing → agreement architecture → independent review → implementation monitoring`

### Design a mediation

`conflict/safety/power assessment → consent and mediator mandate → stakeholder/inclusion plan → facts/issues/process design → joint/private sessions → option generation and reality testing → voluntary agreement → legality/quality gate → follow-up`

### Redesign recurring disputes

`dispute data + access research → actor/power map → prevention opportunities → stepped channels → neutral options + appeal → remedy and anti-retaliation controls → case learning → recurrence/equity review`

## Research anchors and status

Status checked 2026-08-13. Negotiation and mediation names often describe broad schools with local variants. Verify governing law, professional rules, confidentiality, enforceability, safeguarding, and mediator/arbitrator qualifications.

- [Harvard Program on Negotiation](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/negotiation-skills-daily/principled-negotiation-focus-interests-create-value/) anchors principled negotiation, BATNA, interests, criteria, and value creation; training material is not universal efficacy evidence.
- [United Nations Guidance for Effective Mediation](https://peacemaker.un.org/en/documents/united-nations-guidance-effective-mediation) emphasizes preparedness, consent, impartiality, inclusivity, ownership, law, coherence, coordination, and agreement quality.
- Use original DSD, restorative-practice, organizational ombuds, ODR, arbitration, and mediation sources appropriate to the jurisdiction and setting.
- Route collective/public participation to `24`, legal rights and formal investigation to `13`, decision modeling to `08`, and organizational change to `02`.
