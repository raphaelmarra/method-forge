# Sales, revenue operations, and customer success

Use this catalog when a commercial system must identify opportunities, diagnose buyer needs, qualify fit, govern pipeline and forecasts, close defensible agreements, onboard customers, realize value, renew, and expand. Many named sales methods are proprietary; label them and do not confuse popularity with evidence.

## Sales strategy and coverage

| Candidate | Type and output | Use when | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sales-motion design | commercial operating model; self-serve, inside, field, partner, enterprise, hybrid | complexity, risk, contract value, buying process, and service burden differ | copy SaaS labels or add sellers to compensate for weak product/value |
| Ideal Customer Profile — ICP | account-selection artifact | firm need, fit, value, readiness, economics, and exclusions can guide focus | a few wins define universal fit or user/buyer/beneficiary are conflated |
| Territory design | coverage/allocation method | accounts/geographies/workload/potential require fair actionable ownership | historical revenue locks opportunity, workload, and protected-area bias |
| Account segmentation | service/coverage method | accounts merit differentiated acquisition and success motions | revenue alone ignores potential, profitability, needs, risk, and cost-to-serve |
| Named-account planning | strategic account method | complex high-value relationships need objectives, stakeholders, whitespace, risks, and actions | seller wish list lacks customer evidence and mutual value |
| Buying-group mapping | B2B decision-process method | several actors shape problem, criteria, approval, adoption, and renewal | job titles substitute for actual influence, incentives, and relationships |
| Route-to-market design | channel systems method | direct, partner, distributor, marketplace, and retail economics/control differ | channel volume ignores margin, data, enablement, conflict, and service; canonical in `19` |
| Sales capacity model | planning/economic model | quotas, ramp, productivity, cycle, coverage, attrition, and support drive hiring | productivity is assumed immediate/constant or capacity creates demand automatically |
| Quota and incentive design | behavior/economic system | targets, territories, pay mix, thresholds, crediting, and team dependencies need alignment | plans reward harmful selling, sandbagging, channel conflict, or uncontrollable outcomes |

## Discovery, qualification, and opportunity management

| Candidate | Type and output | Use when | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Consultative selling | customer-problem approach | diagnosis and solution fit matter more than transactional presentation | “consulting” disguises predetermined pitch or seller lacks domain competence |
| SPIN Selling | proprietary question framework; situation, problem, implication, need-payoff | complex discovery needs structured questioning | interrogation, leading questions, or minor transactional sale makes overhead harmful |
| Solution selling | sales methodology family | buyer problem, impact, capabilities, proof, and change path must align | solution is forced onto weak/undefined need or custom promises exceed product |
| Challenger Sale | proprietary commercial approach; teach, tailor, take control | credible insight can reframe a complex buyer decision | provocative pitch lacks evidence, trust, or customer context |
| Sandler Selling System | proprietary staged method | qualification, mutual commitments, pain, budget, decision, and post-sell need discipline | rigid scripting/manipulation conflicts with buyer autonomy and evidence |
| BANT | lightweight qualification heuristic; budget, authority, need, timeline | mature inbound opportunity needs quick basic triage | budget/authority emerge later, complex buying group, or early discovery |
| MEDDIC / MEDDPICC | proprietary qualification framework | enterprise opportunities need metrics, economic buyer, criteria/process, pain, champion, competition/paper process | checkbox theater, seller guesses, or framework is used to pressure unfit buyers |
| SPICED | proprietary discovery/qualification framework; situation, pain, impact, critical event, decision | recurring-revenue sale needs linkage from context to urgency and decision | method owner definitions are not verified or labels replace buyer evidence |
| GPCTBA/C&I | proprietary qualification framework | goals, plans, challenges, timing, budget, authority, consequences/implications need coverage | exhaustive acronym use disrupts natural discovery and relevance |
| Jobs-to-be-Done interview for sales | discovery specialization | circumstance, desired progress, barriers, anxieties, and alternatives shape change | sales interview is represented as unbiased market research or used to manufacture urgency |
| Mutual action plan — MAP | buyer–seller coordination artifact | complex evaluation, security, legal, procurement, implementation, and decisions need shared milestones | seller project plan lacks buyer ownership, dates, exit criteria, and dependencies |
| Proof-of-value / pilot design | staged evidence method | material value/technical/implementation uncertainty can be tested in bounded scope | free pilot lacks baseline, success criteria, data, production path, and conversion decision |
| Opportunity review | governance process | evidence, gaps, next decisions, risks, and resources need challenge | manager interrogates seller using stage theater and outcome bias |
| Deal desk | cross-functional decision gate | pricing, terms, risk, capacity, security, finance, and exceptions need coordinated approval | bureaucratic discount approval delays standard low-risk deals |

## Pipeline, forecasting, and revenue operations

| Candidate | Type and output | Use when | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stage-exit criteria | process/assurance artifact | pipeline stages must reflect completed buyer evidence and decisions | seller activity or elapsed time alone advances stage |
| Pipeline coverage | capacity/flow metric | expected conversion, cycle, target, and pipeline sufficiency need monitoring | universal coverage multiple ignores segment, stage, quality, timing, and capacity |
| Funnel / cohort conversion | descriptive method | stage transitions, leakage, time, and segment differences need diagnosis | correlations prove causes or mixed cohorts obscure process changes |
| Sales velocity | flow metric family | opportunities, win rate, value, and cycle time need one directional sensor | composite becomes target, invites splitting/inflation, or hides distributions |
| Weighted pipeline forecast | probability heuristic | stable historical stage conversion offers a baseline | seller-entered probabilities or structural change make weights fictional |
| Category forecast | judgment method; commit, best case, pipeline | manager/seller evidence can distinguish confidence categories | labels lack definitions, calibration, and outcome tracking |
| Bottom-up opportunity forecast | evidence aggregation method | material deals have timing, amount, process, risks, and next-step evidence | totals inherit correlated optimism and hidden slip risk |
| Statistical forecast | predictive method | repeated clean history supports holdout-tested amount/timing estimates | new market/regime, selection changes, sparse wins, or model output becomes commitment |
| Forecast calibration | assurance method | compare predicted probabilities/categories with observed outcomes | only aggregate accuracy is measured and strategic sandbagging remains |
| Revenue waterfall | integrated representation | demand, pipeline, bookings, backlog, revenue recognition, renewal, and expansion need reconciliation | accounting, operational, and sales definitions are mixed |
| Revenue Operations — RevOps | cross-functional operating model | marketing, sales, success, finance, data, process, and technology need shared lifecycle governance | tool administration or central reporting is called RevOps without decision rights |
| Lead/account routing | operational decision system | eligibility, ownership, priority, SLA, capacity, and exceptions need deterministic rules | opaque scores discriminate, duplicate owners, or high score substitutes for fit |
| CRM stage/data contract | data-governance artifact | lifecycle states, required evidence, ownership, timestamps, changes, and definitions must reconcile | mandatory fields produce fabricated data or CRM becomes surveillance |
| Pipeline hygiene review | data/operating control | stale, duplicate, mis-staged, unowned, or impossible opportunities distort decisions | mass deletion hides failure patterns and legitimate long-cycle deals |

## Customer onboarding, success, retention, and expansion

| Candidate | Type and output | Use when | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Customer-success plan | joint value-realization artifact | desired outcomes, measures, stakeholders, milestones, risks, and governance need shared ownership | vendor-only checklist defines customer value or promises unsupported outcomes |
| Onboarding journey / implementation plan | adoption workflow | setup, migration, training, workflow change, validation, and handoff affect time-to-value | orientation call is called onboarding or success depends on unowned customer work |
| Time-to-first-value / time-to-value | experience/outcome metric family | early evidence of meaningful value predicts adoption and reveals friction | trivial activity is mislabeled value or speed sacrifices safe/complete implementation |
| Customer health score | predictive/triage composite | multiple usage, outcome, relationship, support, and financial signals guide review | opaque score becomes truth, target, or automated adverse action without validation |
| Success segmentation | service model | customer complexity, value, need, risk, and economics justify digital/pooled/dedicated motions | contract value alone determines care or low-touch means neglect |
| Adoption playbook | intervention pattern | identifiable barriers and lifecycle states can trigger evidence-based actions | generic reminders address product/service failures or manipulate engagement |
| Voice-of-customer program | mixed-method learning system | recurring needs, experience, decisions, outcomes, and feedback closure need governance | NPS alone or loudest accounts define roadmap |
| Quarterly business review / executive business review | governance cadence | strategic outcomes, evidence, changes, risks, and joint decisions need periodic review | slide ceremony, upsell pitch, or activity report lacks customer decisions |
| Renewal management | commercial/operating process | value evidence, terms, stakeholders, budget, risk, and timeline require proactive coordination | discounts begin without diagnosing value/product/service causes |
| Churn review | causal-learning workflow | cancellations, contraction, nonuse, and preventable mechanisms need triangulation | reason codes/seller narratives are causal truth or blame customer success alone |
| Service recovery | recovery method | failure requires acknowledgement, containment, remedy, explanation, learning, and trust repair | compensation substitutes for fixing recurring cause or creates inconsistent unfairness |
| Expansion readiness | decision gate | demonstrated value, unmet adjacent need, fit, capacity, and stakeholder support justify growth | upsell quota overrides adoption, budget, customer outcome, and implementation capacity |
| Customer advisory board — CAB | structured strategic participation | selected customers can advise strategy, needs, and ecosystem developments | VIP anecdotes become market evidence or board is sales entertainment |

## Assurance and measurement

| Candidate | Type and output | Use when | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Claim–proof matrix | commercial assurance artifact | sellers need permitted claims, evidence, applicability, limitations, and owners | anecdotes, roadmap, or pilot result become universal guarantee |
| Fit/no-fit gate | ethical/economic control | buyer need, capability, risk, economics, and implementation feasibility determine pursuit | quota pressure turns every account into a fit |
| Discount and exception review | control process | nonstandard economics/terms/risk need authority and precedent management | approval focuses only on price, not cost-to-serve and future behavior |
| Win/loss/no-decision research | mixed-method learning | independent buyer evidence can improve strategy, product, and process | seller recollection or only wins/losses exclude status quo and churn |
| Forecast accuracy and bias | measurement pair | errors, calibration, timing, and systematic optimism/sandbagging need learning | accuracy metric punishes honest uncertainty or encourages conservative manipulation |
| Net revenue retention — NRR | cohort economic metric | recurring revenue expansion/contraction/churn need one portfolio sensor | average NRR hides logo loss, price increases, concentration, margin, and causality |
| Gross revenue retention — GRR | retention metric | isolate retained recurring revenue before expansion | revenue definition, cohorts, FX, acquisitions, and contract changes are inconsistent |
| Customer outcome realization | value measurement framework | success should be evidenced in customer operational/business/user outcomes | vendor usage or satisfaction is called realized value without baseline and attribution |
| Sales-practice compliance review | legal/ethics assurance | consent, claims, pricing, discrimination, bribery, privacy, recording, and contracts matter | training completion replaces behavior/evidence and jurisdiction review |

## Composition patterns

### Run a complex B2B opportunity

`ICP/fit → buying group + discovery → quantified problem/impact → qualification evidence → solution/claim proof → MAP + pilot if needed → security/legal/procurement → deal desk → agreement → onboarding/success handoff`

### Build a reliable revenue forecast

`stage definitions + exits → CRM evidence contract → cohort conversion/cycle distributions → opportunity bottoms-up → statistical baseline → manager challenge → calibration/bias review → scenarios and capacity actions`

### Operate customer success

`desired customer outcomes → onboarding/time-to-value → adoption and relationship evidence → health triage → success interventions → QBR decisions → renewal/expansion gate → churn/service-recovery learning → product/process feedback`

## Research anchors and status

Status checked 2026-08-13. SPIN, Challenger, Sandler, MEDDIC/MEDDPICC, SPICED, and related methods have proprietary owners or training ecosystems; verify owner definitions and independently evaluate evidence.

- Use primary owner materials for named proprietary methods, but do not treat promotional case studies as comparative efficacy evidence.
- Use CRM/platform documentation only for tool semantics; pipeline, forecast, and customer-success validity must come from the organization's decision definitions and observed outcomes.
- Verify consumer/commercial law, privacy, recording consent, anti-bribery, competition, accessibility, sector claims, pricing, and contract authority through `13`.
- Route market/offer/GTM to `19`, financial economics to `12`, behavior/adoption to `22`, negotiation to `23`, and causal measurement to `08`.
