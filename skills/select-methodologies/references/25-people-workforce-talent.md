## Work and workforce planning

| Candidate | Type and output | Use when | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Strategic workforce planning | planning backbone; demand, supply, gaps, scenarios, actions | strategy changes future capacity, skills, location, or employment models | headcount extrapolation ignores work redesign, productivity, uncertainty, and finance |
| Workforce segmentation | planning method | roles differ in strategic value, scarcity, risk, or employment relationship | segment labels justify inequitable treatment or reduce people to cost |
| Job analysis | foundational research method; tasks, context, KSAOs, outputs, hazards | selection, training, evaluation, pay, or redesign needs defensible work evidence | copied job descriptions or incumbent opinion define future work |
| Work analysis / task analysis | decomposition method | workflows, cognitive demands, coordination, and errors need finer understanding | atomized tasks erase judgment, teamwork, variability, and whole-job outcomes |
| Critical incident technique | behavior/requirements elicitation | effective and ineffective episodes can identify role requirements | memorable incidents substitute for representative frequency and base rates |
| Job architecture | organizational representation; families, levels, scope, progression | inconsistent titles, levels, pay, mobility, and skills need common structure | bureaucracy freezes evolving work or levels become status inflation |
| Role charter / accountability design | governance artifact | purpose, outcomes, decisions, interfaces, and constraints are unclear | static description substitutes for process and team redesign |
| Span-of-control analysis | organization-design technique | managerial load differs by complexity, standardization, geography, and capability | universal ratio is imposed without work-system evidence |
| Workload and capacity modeling | operations/workforce method | demand, service levels, effort, schedules, shrinkage, and variability determine staffing | average workload ignores peaks, queues, skill mix, absence, and quality |
| Skills-based workforce planning | capability approach | work can be decomposed into evolving skills and internal mobility matters | unvalidated skill taxonomies, surveillance, or decontextualized micro-skills drive decisions |
| Scenario-based workforce planning | adaptive planning method | technology, demand, policy, or strategy creates multiple plausible workforce futures | one scenario becomes forecast or no actions/triggers follow |
| Sociotechnical work design | joint-optimization methodology | people, process, technology, incentives, autonomy, and feedback must be designed together | automation is predetermined and “change management” merely seeks compliance |

## Competencies and selection

| Candidate | Type and output | Use when | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Competency modeling | capability model; observable behaviors and proficiency levels | shared role expectations, development, assessment, and mobility need structure | generic values, personality, or exhaustive lists lack job linkage and validation |
| Skills taxonomy / ontology | structured representation | skills must map across roles, learning, supply, and demand | vendor ontology becomes truth or proficiency/context is absent |
| Structured interview | standardized selection method | job-related questions, anchored scoring, trained assessors, and comparable evidence are feasible | informal rapport or protected traits influence scoring; one interview is sole evidence |
| Situational judgment test — SJT | assessment method | job-relevant scenarios can sample judgment under standardized conditions | scenarios/correct answers lack criterion validity or cultural fairness |
| Work-sample test | performance assessment | candidates can safely perform representative tasks with consistent scoring | unpaid productive labor, inaccessible simulation, confidentiality, or narrow task overfit |
| Assessment center | multi-exercise/multi-assessor method | complex managerial/interpersonal competencies justify high-cost observation | exercises/assessors lack standardization, validation, and job relevance |
| Cognitive ability assessment | psychometric method | complex learning/reasoning predicts relevant performance and adverse impact is governed | one score defines merit, accommodations absent, or legal/professional requirements are ignored |
| Personality assessment | psychometric supplement | validated job-relevant traits add incremental information | clinical instruments, typologies, faking, or weak validity drive exclusion |
| Biodata / experience inventory | structured background method | past job-relevant experiences can be scored consistently | proxies for socioeconomic/protected traits or unverifiable claims create unfairness |
| Realistic job preview | expectation-alignment method | candidates need balanced evidence about work, conditions, and trade-offs | marketing conceals burdens or deters protected groups through avoidable barriers |
| Selection battery / compensatory model | decision system | multiple valid predictors add distinct information and trade-offs are acceptable | fatal safety/licensing requirement can be compensated by unrelated strengths |
| Multiple-hurdle selection | gated decision process | legal, safety, credential, or minimum capability gates precede costly assessment | arbitrary early hurdle creates adverse impact and discards useful evidence |
| Adverse-impact analysis | fairness/selection assurance | selection rates and validity must be examined across groups | statistical parity alone proves fairness or small samples are overinterpreted |

## Performance, rewards, and development

| Candidate | Type and output | Use when | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Performance-management cycle | governance backbone; expectations, check-ins, evidence, review, development | ongoing contribution and growth need structured dialogue | annual rating ritual is tied mechanically to pay without evidence or appeal |
| Goals + behavior/competency model | balanced expectation method | both results and how work is performed materially matter | behaviors excuse missed outcomes or outcomes excuse unsafe/unethical conduct |
| Behaviorally anchored rating scales — BARS | performance-scale development method | observable job behaviors can improve rating clarity | anchors are generic, outdated, or used as exhaustive job definition |
| 360-degree feedback | development feedback method | multiple relationships observe different leadership/interpersonal behaviors | anonymous scores determine pay/promotion or retaliation and low rater literacy exist |
| Calibration meeting | cross-manager review process | rating standards and evidence need consistency across teams | forced distribution, politics, and unrecorded changes override job evidence |
| Objectives and Key Results — OKRs | goal-setting framework | aligned outcome learning benefits from periodic transparent goals | individual performance/pay is calculated mechanically from scores; canonical card in `02` |
| Individual development plan — IDP | development artifact | role goals, gaps, experiences, learning, support, and evidence need ownership | course list substitutes for practice, feedback, opportunity, and career path |
| 70–20–10 | development allocation heuristic | prompt a mix of experience, relationships, and formal learning | percentages are treated as evidence-based universal prescription |
| Deliberate practice | skill-development method | decomposable performance, feedback, stretch, repetition, and coaching exist | ambiguous creative/leadership outcomes lack reliable practice tasks |
| Career pathways / lattices | mobility framework | employees and organization need visible progression and lateral routes | one linear path privileges management or promises roles that do not exist |
| Succession planning | continuity/talent process | critical roles need readiness, development, risk, and transition plans | secret replacement charts entrench similarity or labels become permanent |
| Talent review / nine-box | portfolio heuristic | performance and future-role potential need discussion with evidence | subjective “potential” and forced boxes drive irreversible decisions |
| Job evaluation | internal-equity method | role value must inform grade/pay architecture consistently | evaluates incumbent worth, ignores market/law, or opaque factors encode bias |
| Total rewards design | compensation/benefits framework | pay, benefits, recognition, flexibility, and careers must align with workforce strategy | benchmark median replaces affordability, internal equity, behavior effects, and employee needs |

## Teams, experience, inclusion, and relations

| Candidate | Type and output | Use when | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Team charter | team governance artifact | purpose, membership, decisions, interfaces, norms, and review need agreement | workshop document is never used or conflicts with formal authority |
| Team effectiveness diagnostic | diagnostic family | goals, roles, processes, relationships, context, and outcomes need evidence | proprietary score labels team without behavioral and operational data |
| Psychological-safety assessment | climate measure/lens | speaking up, learning, error reporting, and interpersonal risk matter | survey score blames individuals or excuses low standards/accountability |
| Employee listening system | mixed-method capability | experience, barriers, risks, and ideas need continuous representative feedback | pulse-survey volume replaces action, anonymity, sampling, and feedback closure |
| Employee journey mapping | experience representation | moments across joining, work, growth, change, and exit need cross-functional redesign | generic emotion map replaces segmentation, process, and outcome evidence |
| Organizational network analysis — ONA | relational analysis method | collaboration, advice, bottlenecks, isolation, and informal structure matter | surveillance, reidentification, centrality-as-performance, or context-free interpretation |
| Diversity, equity, inclusion, and belonging assessment | diagnostic/assurance family | representation, access, treatment, progression, voice, and outcomes may differ | awareness training substitutes for structural evidence and accountable action |
| Accessibility and accommodations process | rights/operating method | workers need individualized adjustments and barrier removal | medical proof is overcollected or accommodation becomes discretionary favor |
| Stay interview | retention-research method | understand valued conditions, barriers, and preventable departure risk | manager coercion, false promises, or anecdote becomes population truth |
| Exit analysis | retrospective research method | patterns in departure, experience, management, role, and labor market need learning | exit reason dropdown is causal truth or only voluntary leavers are studied |
| Grievance and speak-up system | governance/process family | concerns need safe intake, triage, investigation/referral, protection, remedy, and learning | channel count substitutes for trust, due process, and anti-retaliation |

## People analytics and assurance

| Candidate | Type and output | Use when | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| People metric tree | measurement/governance artifact | workforce inputs, processes, experience, capability, behavior, and outcomes need decision linkage | dashboard metrics become targets or imply causality |
| ISO 30414 human-capital reporting | reporting/governance standard | comparable internal/external disclosure across workforce areas is required | disclosure framework is used as talent strategy or individual evaluation method |
| Workforce-flow analysis | longitudinal method | hiring, movement, promotion, absence, and exit patterns require cohort/transition views | snapshots hide exposure time and eligibility or correlations prove discrimination mechanism |
| Utility analysis | economic decision method | selection/training/workforce interventions need expected value and uncertainty | monetization erases rights, dignity, safety, and distribution |
| Validation study | psychometric/assurance method | assessment or performance measure must show job relevance, reliability, validity, and fairness | vendor validity generalizes without local job/population/use evidence |
| Algorithmic employment impact assessment | high-impact assurance process | automated screening, ranking, scheduling, monitoring, or decisions affect work rights | model accuracy alone authorizes use or human review is nominal |
| Privacy and proportionality review | hard gate | workforce data collection, inference, retention, and access may be intrusive | employment consent is assumed freely given or “analytics” overrides purpose limitation |
| Independent appeal / review | procedural assurance | consequential decisions need explanation, correction, accommodation, and challenge | reviewer repeats the same opaque score and lacks authority |

## Composition patterns

### Build a defensible selection system

`job/work analysis → critical requirements + accommodations → valid predictor longlist → structured battery/hurdles → scoring and assessor training → pilot/validation → adverse-impact + privacy review → documented decision → appeal → outcome monitoring`

### Plan the workforce under uncertainty

`strategy/scenarios → work and capability demand → current supply/flows → gap/criticality → build/buy/borrow/bot/redesign options → economics + rights gates → staged actions → leading indicators → refresh`

### Improve a role and employee experience

`work evidence + employee listening → task/journey/network analysis → sociotechnical causes → co-designed work/role changes → workload and safety checks → pilot → operational + experience outcomes → scale/adapt`

## Research anchors and status

Status checked 2026-08-13. Verify labor, equality, accessibility, data-protection, collective-bargaining, professional, and automated-decision rules for the jurisdiction.

- [ISO 30414:2025](https://www.iso.org/standard/30414) is the current second edition for human-capital reporting and disclosure; ISO 30414:2018 is withdrawn.
- Consult the current ISO 30400 family for vocabulary, recruitment, workforce planning, diversity/inclusion, learning, and assessment-related standards; use authorized standards for conformity claims.
- Use current professional personnel-selection standards and original psychometric literature for job analysis, validation, structured interviews, work samples, assessment centers, and adverse-impact analysis.
- Route organization design/change to `02`, learning design to `18`, operations/capacity to `03`, negotiation/grievance design to `23`, and law/ethics/AI to `13`/`09`.
